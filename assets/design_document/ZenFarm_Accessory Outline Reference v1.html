Coordinate conventionsame grid logic as the animal reference

X
Width — left ↔ right

Side-to-side axis. Keep accessories centered so X midpoint can align to the animal head socket.

Y
Height — bottom ↑ top

Layer 0 is the contact or floating base. Build upward in MagicaVoxel.

Z
Depth — back ↔ front

0 is the back. Max Z is the front, matching the animal face direction.

Voxel outline rulesfake black edges directly in the model

1
Dark bottom contact

Make Y=0 mostly outline/shadow colour. This separates the accessory from the animal head below it.

2
Dark outer shell

Use shadow voxels on min/max X, back edges, side faces, and lower rims. Keep bright colours 1 voxel inward.

3
Bigger front icons

Jewels, stars, crystal highlights, and moon glints should be 2-5 voxels total, not single dots.

MagicaVoxel tipssmall props with readable outlines

01 · CANVAS

Use a slightly thicker box

Crown: 8 × 8 × 6. Wizard Hat: 7 × 7 × 8. Lotus Halo: 7 × 7 × 5. Crystal Sprout: 6 × 6 × 7. Moon Charm: 7 × 5 × 7. Use the extra space for dark rims.

02 · OUTLINE

Never outline every voxel

Only outline the silhouette: bottom rim, side extremes, back edge, and front icon border. Full checkerboard outlines become noisy in game.

03 · READABILITY

Use base + shadow + highlight

Each accessory uses one dark outline colour, one main colour, and one highlight/accent colour. This reads like a black edge after export.
