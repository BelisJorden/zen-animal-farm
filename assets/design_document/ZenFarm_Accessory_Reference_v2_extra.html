Attachment rulesmake them easy to place above any animal

1
Center the footprint

Keep the bottom layer symmetrical around the model center. For even widths, align the two center voxels over the head.

2
Preserve face direction

Put jewels, stars, openings, or crescent details on max Z so the front view reads clearly.

3
Leave a tiny air gap

In-game, place most accessories 0.5-1 voxel above the head unless they are meant to wrap around it.

01 · CANVAS

Use a tight box

Crown: 8 × 8 × 6. Wizard Hat: 7 × 7 × 8. Lotus Halo: 7 × 7 × 5. Crystal Sprout: 6 × 6 × 7. Moon Charm: 7 × 5 × 7. Cherry Blossom: 7 × 7 × 5. Lantern: 5 × 5 × 7. Rainbow Arc: 9 × 5 × 3. Fox Mask: 7 × 7 × 3. Campfire: 5 × 5 × 5.

02 · SOCKET

Export from centered origin

Keep the accessory centered in the canvas, then use one shared attach offset in the game for each animal size class.

03 · READABILITY

One strong icon per prop

Each accessory should have one obvious shape: crown points, cone hat, halo ring, crystal cluster, or crescent. Tiny decorations are secondary.
